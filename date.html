Solar Hijri, Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri date converter

Universal Convertor synchronizes Solar Hijri (Jalali), Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri dates in the browser. Change any field and the other calendars update locally. Nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Is the date conversion performed entirely in my browser?

Yes. The date converter uses local calendar algorithms and browser Intl APIs. Dates are not sent to a server.

How accurate are the Solar Hijri and Lunar Hijri conversions?

Solar Hijri uses the 33-year leap cycle. Lunar Hijri follows the Umm al-Qura astronomical standard, calculated on the device.

How does the converter calculate leap years?

Leap years are calculated for Solar Hijri, Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri at the same time. A badge marks a leap year on the active calendar.
