Free client-side image, document, PDF, and date converter

Universal Convertor processes PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC, SVG, BMP, ICO, TIFF, PSD, GIF, JSON, CSV, TSV, Markdown, HTML, XML, and plain text entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. A separate page converts Solar Hijri, Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri dates.

Frequently asked questions

Is Universal Convertor really 100% free with no conversion limits?

Yes. Universal Convertor is free with no file-size limits, daily caps, or subscriptions. Decoding, resizing, and encoding run in the browser, so there is no upload and no server storage bill.

Are my photos or documents uploaded to any external server?

No. Files stay in browser memory on your device. They are not sent to a server, cloud bucket, or third-party API.

Which image and document formats are supported?

Images: PNG, JPG, JPEG, JFIF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF where the browser can decode them, SVG, BMP, ICO, TIFF, PSD, and GIF. Documents: Markdown, HTML, plain text, XML, CSV, TSV, and JSON. You can also merge images into a PDF or download a ZIP.

Can I convert dates between Solar Hijri, Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri?

Yes. The date converter at /date synchronizes Solar Hijri, Gregorian, and Lunar Hijri calendars locally in the browser.
